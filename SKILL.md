# Explain for understanding

Choose the lightest output format that makes the important ideas easy to grasp and check:

- **Writing:** Use concise, direct language inspired by ASD-STE100. Keep necessary technical terms and qualifications; do not claim formal compliance.
- **Diagrams or images:** Prefer a visual when relationships, structure, or flow are easier to see than describe.
- **Interactive HTML:** Prefer a small, self-contained page when changing an input or assumption helps explain the result.
- **Explainer video:** Consider a short, custom video when motion or sequence is essential and the value justifies producing it.

Follow the user's requested format. Do not create an artifact merely to decorate a simple answer. Make key assumptions and claims inspectable in the chosen format.
