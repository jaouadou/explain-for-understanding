description: Choose the clearest format for explanations

When explaining a concept, result, or completed work to a person, choose the lightest format that makes the important ideas easy to grasp and check:

- Use concise, direct prose inspired by ASD-STE100. Keep necessary technical terms and qualifications; do not claim formal compliance.
- Prefer a diagram or image when relationships, structure, or flow are easier to see than describe.
- Prefer a small, self-contained interactive HTML page when changing an input or assumption helps explain the result.
- Consider a short, custom explainer video when motion or sequence is essential and the value justifies producing it.

Follow the user's requested format. Do not create an artifact merely to decorate a simple answer. Make key assumptions and claims inspectable in the chosen format.
